How the Deletion Process Works

  1. Submit Request: You provide your registered 10-digit mobile number or Google account email.
  2. Verification: To safeguard against unauthorized deletion, our store team may verify ownership via SMS or phone confirmation.
  3. Data Removal: Once verified, your account credentials, profile details, and session tokens are permanently wiped from our database within 7 business days.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

✅ Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted:

  • Your customer account profile (Name, Mobile Number, Email Address).
  • Authentication credentials and Firebase tokens.
  • Saved grocery carts and browsing preferences.
  • Push notification device tokens.

📋 Data Retained for Legal Compliance:

Historical financial transaction records, order logs, and generated retail invoices from past completed orders are retained solely to comply with applicable statutory, tax, and accounting record-keeping requirements in India. These retained records are not used for any marketing or ongoing profiling purposes.
